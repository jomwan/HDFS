📊 Sentiment Trend Analysis

A massive 62.7% of passenger feedback is classified as negative, whereas positive feedback is a mere 16.1%. This demonstrates a severe systemic negative sentiment baseline in U.S. domestic aviation customer service, reflecting widespread passenger frustration during the analysis window.

  • Negative Tweets: 9,178
  • Neutral Tweets: 3,099
  • Positive Tweets: 2,363

⚠️ Top Operational Failure Modes

The most frequent complaints, analyzed via Spark, show that Customer Service Issue is the primary driver with 2,910 incidents. This outweighs flight delays, meaning that passengers' frustration spikes when facing unhelpful customer service channels.

  • 1. Customer Service Issue: 2,910
  • 2. Late Flight: 1,665
  • 3. Can't Tell: 1,190

🛠️ Operational Recommendations

1. Expand Front-line Support Capacity: Given that "Customer Service Issue" is the number one cause of negative sentiment (2,910 counts), U.S. carriers must drastically invest in scaling digital chat channels and customer response systems.

2. Optimize Real-Time Alert Buffers: As shown in the daily volume analysis, sentiment volume peaked heavily on Feb 23 (3,515 tweets). Operational teams need dynamic queue staffing models aligned with adverse weather or operational events to handle feedback spikes.
